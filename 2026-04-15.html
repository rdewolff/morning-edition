Introspective Diffusion: A New Architecture for Language Models

Researchers have introduced Introspective Diffusion Language Models — a hybrid architecture that combines the parallel generation capabilities of diffusion models with the reasoning depth of autoregressive transformers. Instead of generating tokens one-by-one, these models "diffuse" entire sequences and then introspectively refine them. Early results show significant speed improvements while maintaining quality. For anyone building AI-powered text tools (hint hint, Whisperit), this is worth watching closely.
▲ 250 points 💬 45 comments introspective-diffusion.github.io →
