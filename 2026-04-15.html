Introspective Diffusion: A New Architecture That Lets Language Models "Think Visually"

A new paper introduces Introspective Diffusion Language Models — a hybrid architecture that combines the generative power of diffusion models with the reasoning of language models. Instead of generating text token by token, the model can "diffuse" through multiple possible completions simultaneously, then converge on the best one. Early results show improved reasoning and more coherent long-form generation. This could be the next architectural paradigm after transformers.
